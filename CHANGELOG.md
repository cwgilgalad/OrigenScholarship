## v1.0.0 (2026-10-15)

The first release: the research, finished, and the canvas it feeds.

- **The survey** (`research/REPORT.md`): Origen's life and first censure, the controversy around 400, the documents of 543 and 553, whether the condemned doctrines are Origen's, how binding the condemnations are in each tradition, whether they were just, and where the debate stands in 2026.
- **The claim check** (`research/claims.md`): 63 claims, each with its source and how far it was checked. Four were corrected and none turned out false.
- **The balance audit** (`research/balance-audit.md`), in two passes. The second followed up the first's gaps: Hart is quoted from his own essay, Jenkins's credentials are confirmed, Guillaumont, Edwards and Clark rest on better sources, and three more voices on the traditional side (Green, Maximov, Telea) were added.
- **The canvas** (`canvas/`): the Origen Design Showcase gains a fourth section, *Was Origen condemned?*, drawn from the report. Two details elsewhere on the page were brought into line with it: Origen's death at Tyre is now marked as tradition, and the commentary list no longer calls him condemned as a heretic without qualification.
